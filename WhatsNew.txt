What's New in This Version of Shotbot

• iPhone Duo support — frame screenshots from both of the Duo's screens, in portrait and landscape
• Inner screen: choose Open or Partially Open, which folds the frame along the hinge with a soft shadow
• Outer screen: choose Closed or Open
• Pick a frame color, such as Night Sky or Star White, from the new toolbar menu. Shotbot remembers your choice for next time
• Frame colors are now available for more devices, and your color and view choices carry over to the share extension
• Set a different color or view for a single screenshot
• The share extension now frames screenshots that fit several devices, such as the iPhone 16 Pro, 17 and 18 Pro, using the device you chose in the app
